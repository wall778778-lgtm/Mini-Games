Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy describes how the Mini Games website handles information when you browse the site and play its browser games. The site does not require an account and does not include a visitor registration or comment form.

Game play and website logs

The games process player input and calculate scores in your browser. The current game pages do not send game scores to a Mini Games account or game server. Hosting and network services may process technical request information, such as the page requested, time of request, browser or device details, and IP address, for delivery, security, and troubleshooting. The hosting provider's own privacy terms also apply.

Advertising and cookies

Google AdSense code is installed on game pages so ads can be served if and when Google approves the site. When advertising is active, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers and process information such as your IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, and interactions with ads. This may be used to deliver, measure, and protect advertising, and to personalize ads where permitted by your settings and applicable law.

For details, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. Review ad personalization at My Ad Center. Browser settings can also limit or remove cookies, though this may affect some site features.

Consent choices

Where consent is required, advertising choices should be presented through a consent management platform configured for the site. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may see a consent message before personalized advertising is used. Use the message or consent controls provided by Google to review your choice.

Children

Mini Games is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The site does not ask players to provide personal information to play. If you believe a child sent personal information to the site owner by email, contact us so the request can be reviewed.

Your privacy requests

Privacy rights depend on your location and the services involved. For a request about information sent directly to Mini Games, contact stootdeveloper@gmail.com. For information processed by Google, use Google's privacy and advertising controls linked above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, its services, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top shows the most recent revision.
