Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Introduction

Mini Games (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides free browser games and respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Anonymous usage data: aggregate analytics about how games are played (levels, scores, session lengths) for improving gameplay and fixing bugs.
  • Device and browser information: device type, screen size, browser type, and operating system to ensure games run smoothly across devices.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: to remember preferences and to serve personalized ads through our advertising partners.

Cookies and Advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience and to show ads. Third-party advertising providers (including Google AdSense) may use cookies and other identifiers to serve personalized ads based on your browsing activity and interactions with our site and other websites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google’s Ads Settings: https://adssettings.google.com. Blocking cookies may affect site functionality and game performance.

Ads and Disclosure

This site displays ads managed by third-party providers. Ads help us keep games free. We do not control the content of these ads and are not responsible for them. If you have questions about the ads you see, please contact the ad provider directly.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (for analytics, hosting, advertising) that collect information according to their own policies. Links to third-party policies are provided by the service providers (for example, Google’s privacy policy for AdSense and Analytics).

Children

Our games are intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, contact us and we will take steps to delete that information.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise those rights, contact us at the email below.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at: admin@minigames.example

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when changes were made.
